## Numerical scoring boundary

Correctness uses supplied human source-content judgments of the frozen required
facts, numerical values within the existing ±1% tolerance, units and direction,
plus the exact expected status. The previous numeric-token helper is retained
only as a diagnostic: final P1V2-09 expresses a decline of 1.6 as a signed change
of -1.6. Its literal target-token check fails despite the numerical meaning
matching the reference. This is disclosed rather than silently dropping that
answer. No numeric target, reference, inference rule or human label was changed
for this diagnostic. Baseline P1V2-11 is different: the answer is substantively
incomplete/wrong and the correction was explicitly confirmed by the user.

This differs from blindly applying the legacy token-presence gate; consumers
must use this disclosed source-content scoring method with these review results.

## Interpretation and limits

The final system has higher reviewed answer correctness, retrieval recall and
citation support in this fixed development evaluation, yet some answers and
many emitted citations still fail review. Groundedness, answer correctness and
citation support are distinct metrics with different units and denominators.

The final answers judged incorrect are: P1V2-06, P1V2-10, P1V2-11, P1V2-13, P1V2-20, P1V2-22.
The review notes identify forecast/outturn confusion, slope/intercept confusion,
missing inflation-series details, unemployment/participation comparison errors,
a fabricated revision and an incomplete erratum answer. Preserve these failures.

The final latency includes structured-evidence and session-memory answers that
avoid a VLM call; it is not model-only decoding speed. Questions/rubrics were
fixed, but development fixes were informed by prior failures. These scores are
not an independent holdout result, a Kaggle score, or a guarantee of hidden-set
accuracy. The final tagged Kaggle execution/selection remains a separate task.

See `provenance.json` for input hashes and `metrics.json` for all per-case counts
and the retained token-check diagnostic. The earlier `summary.json` and the
original groundedness aggregation retain their historical null fields; this
completed record supplements them rather than rewriting original observations.
